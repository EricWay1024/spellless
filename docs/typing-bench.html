How it is scored. Not letter against letter in the same position — drop one character early on and every letter after it would read as wrong, which measures nothing. Your line and the model are aligned by the fewest edits that turn one into the other, and what is counted is how many letters survive that alignment. So a missing letter costs one edit, not the rest of the line. The clock runs from your first keystroke and includes the time spent choosing from an input method's candidate list; what it credits is the text that arrived, which is the only comparison worth making.

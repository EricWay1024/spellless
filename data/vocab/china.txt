# Chinese provinces, and words English has borrowed without the corpus
# noticing.
#
# The cities were already there -- twenty-three of twenty-five checked,
# Shenzhen and Chengdu and Harbin included -- because Google Books has read
# about them. The provinces mostly were not, which is the sort of asymmetry a
# books corpus produces: places are written about, administrative divisions are
# filled in on forms.
#
# Romanisation is pinyin without tone marks, which is what anyone types.
# `Shanxi` and `Shaanxi` are two different provinces and both are here; the
# doubled `a` is the only thing distinguishing them in this spelling, and
# getting it wrong is a mistake the matcher should be able to repair rather
# than one it should make.

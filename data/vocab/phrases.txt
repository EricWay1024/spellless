# Word groups that behave as one word when you type them.
#
# "in front of" is three words to the dictionary and one thing to the writer.
# Typing it as "infrontof" and picking it once is fewer keystrokes and fewer
# decisions than typing three words and choosing three times -- and, because
# these go through the same matching as everything else, "infrntof" still
# finds it.
#
# The lookup key is the letters alone; what lands in the document is the entry
# as written here, spaces and all.  Typing the words separately still works
# exactly as before -- this is an addition, not a replacement.
#
# Keep the key unambiguous: it must not be a word in its own right, and it
# should not be so short that it collides with ordinary typing.

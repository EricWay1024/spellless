#!rank 55000
# Words that are only ever written with a capital.
#
# The dictionary is lowercase throughout, so it cannot say that "english" is
# never a word.  Capitalisation is otherwise recovered from how you typed --
# "Mathe" gives "Mathematics" -- which is no help when you typed it in lower
# case in the first place.  Each entry here is indexed under its lowercase
# letters and commits as written.
#
# The bar for adding one: is the lowercase spelling wrong in *every* context?
# That is why "March", "May", "August", "Polish", "Turkey" and "Frank" are
# absent -- each is an ordinary word as well, and listing it would make the
# ordinary word unreachable except through the literal candidate.
#
# "China" is here despite "china" the porcelain, on the grounds that one of
# those is overwhelmingly more likely in prose; typing "china" still offers the
# lowercase word, it simply does not lead.

# Literature, film, art and music: the names that come up in conversation.
#
# This is the pack where collisions bite hardest, because artists are called
# King, Cash, Grant, Cruise, Lee, Wood, Price, Glass, Frost, Hardy, Lynch,
# Reich, Cage, Bowie, Eco and Hopper, and films are called Alien, Matrix,
# Avatar, Psycho, Vertigo, Titanic, Parasite, Inception and Barbie.  Two
# answers, both used below:
#
#   * a person keeps their first name -- `Stephen King`, `Johnny Cash`,
#     `Philip Glass` -- which is one selection and cannot collide;
#   * a film whose title is simply a common noun is left out.  `Matrix` is a
#     mathematical object, `Price` is the 97th word in the language, and no
#     amount of cleverness makes those worth taking.
#
# Not included, because the dictionary already spells them: blockbuster,
# cinematography, screenplay, protagonist, antagonist, narrative, allegory,
# metaphor, motif, trope, soliloquy, sonnet, stanza, couplet, iambic,
# pentameter, novella, memoir, bildungsroman -- and Netflix and Venice, which
# other files carry.

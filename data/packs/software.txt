#!rank 40000
# Computer science and software engineering: the vocabulary the base corpus is
# too old or too literary to have.
#
# A pack rather than shipped vocabulary.  data/vocab/technology.txt carries the
# part everybody needs -- API, CLI, JSON, macOS, GitHub -- and this is the part
# you only want if you write software: syscalls, spectral vocabulary from
# concurrency, the names attached to algorithms, and the tools.
#
# Every entry here was checked against the shipped dictionary: 86 candidates
# were dropped because they are already in it and spelled correctly (monad,
# functor, currying, idempotent, semaphore, deadlock, refactor, boilerplate,
# telemetry, generics, lifetimes, Kubernetes, Nginx, Redis).  A pack that
# repeats the dictionary is noise.
#
# Names are here only where the lowercase spelling is not itself a word, since
# a personal entry *replaces* a spelling: Dijkstra and Knuth yes, Bloom and
# Raft and Prim no -- those would cost you the flower, the boat and the
# adjective.  They appear as phrases instead, where the key cannot collide.

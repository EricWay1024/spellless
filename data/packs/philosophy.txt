# Philosophy: the names, the isms, and the terms of art.
#
# A pack rather than shipped vocabulary, like topology.txt: excellent if you
# read philosophy and clutter if you do not.
#
# Not included, because the dictionary already spells them: epistemology,
# ontology, metaphysics, phenomenology, hermeneutics, teleology, deontology,
# utilitarianism, existentialism, nihilism, solipsism, empiricism, rationalism,
# idealism, materialism, physicalism, functionalism, dualism, monism,
# determinism, externalism, relativism, realism, nominalism, essentialism,
# naturalism, qualia, intentionality, counterfactual, defeasible, syllogism,
# intension, extension, transcendental, dialectic, aporia, logos, ethos,
# pathos, praxis, nous, arete, positivism, pragmatism, structuralism,
# deconstruction, intuitionism, formalism, platonism, and Berkeley.
#
# Ten philosophers appear under their full names because their surnames are
# ordinary words: a bare `Mill`, `Singer`, `Block`, `Foot`, `Field`, `Bacon`,
# `Hare`, `Wright`, `Hacking` or `Sober` would lead over the mill, the singer
# and the foot.  The full name is also one selection rather than two.

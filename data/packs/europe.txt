#!rank 40000
# Travelling in Europe: airports by name, the airlines and railways, and the
# cities and regions the corpus has only in lower case.
#
# A pack rather than shipped vocabulary, for the same reason china.txt and
# britain.txt are: it is a fact about where you go, not about English.
#
# Airports are their names rather than their codes.  Codes were tried and are
# not worth it: a third of the useful ones collide with ordinary words -- HER,
# MAN, GOT, INN, MAD, HAM, DUB, BUD, NAP, VIE -- and `her` is the 132nd word in
# the language.  A capital no longer replaces a lowercase reading, so nothing
# would have broken, but `HER` leading over `her` is not a trade worth making
# for an airport code you can also just type.
#
# `LOT` and `TAP` are airlines and also the 839th and 6,468th words, so they
# appear as phrases; `Nice` and `Split` are cities and also ordinary words, and
# are left out entirely -- add them yourself if you are going.
#
# Not included, because the dictionary already spells them correctly: Amsterdam
# Athens Barcelona Basel Berlin Brussels Budapest Copenhagen Cologne Edinburgh
# Florence Frankfurt Geneva Hamburg Helsinki Istanbul Lisbon Madrid Milan Munich
# Naples Oslo Porto Prague Rotterdam Seville Stockholm Valencia Venice Vienna
# Warsaw Zurich Norwegian Swiss Austrian, and every ordinary travel word from
# `passport` and `layover` to `funicular` and `turnstile`.
